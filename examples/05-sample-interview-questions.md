# Illustrative Interview Questions

Use one question at a time. Answer in your own words and cite evidence from your own project.

1. Walk me through the request path from client to Pod.
2. How do a Kubernetes Deployment and Service differ?
3. How do readiness and liveness probes affect workload behavior?
4. What evidence would you collect if a Service had no endpoints?
5. What does Helm add to the deployment workflow, and what does it not solve?
6. How does the CI workflow prove that a failing test blocks a change?
7. What security trade-offs did you make for the container image?
8. Which parts are local lab work, and what would need to change for production use?

These are practice prompts, not claims about any specific employer's interview process.
