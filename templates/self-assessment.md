# Learner Self-Assessment

Use this as a planning aid, not as a credential or formal skills test. Rate **current demonstrated proficiency** separately from **where the evidence came from**. A high rating does not imply professional experience.

## Proficiency scale

| Rating | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | No exposure or not yet able to explain the basics |
| 1 | Can explain basic concepts; little or no independent practice |
| 2 | Can complete basic tasks with a guide, examples, or troubleshooting help |
| 3 | Can complete representative tasks independently and explain key trade-offs |
| 4 | Can handle more complex or ambiguous tasks, troubleshoot failures systematically, and explain design choices and limitations |

Use the highest rating you can support with a concrete example. If uncertain, choose the lower rating and add a note. This is a self-rating, not an independently verified assessment.

## Evidence type (choose one or more)
- **Professional:** performed as part of a paid role; describe your actual responsibility and outcome.
- **Lab/Personal Project:** practiced in a lab, course, homelab, or personal portfolio project.
- **Planned:** not yet completed; do not present as existing experience.
- **None supplied:** no concrete example or artifact described yet.

## Evidence strength
- **None:** no example supplied.
- **Description:** can describe what was done, but there is no inspectable artifact.
- **Artifact:** code, configuration, runbook, diagram, test output, or other inspectable evidence exists.
- **Reproducible:** another person can follow documented steps and verify the result.
